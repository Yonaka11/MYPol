App Support

Welcome to the support page for the VCU Pharmacy Tool. This application is currently in Beta (TestFlight).

Beta Testers: If the app crashes, a log is automatically sent to the developer via TestFlight. You do not need to email us about crashes unless you have specific details to add.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. I cannot log in.

Access is restricted to authorized personnel. If you are reviewing this app for TestFlight or internal testing, please use the Demo Credentials provided in your invitation email or the "Review Notes" section.

2. The floor plan data looks incorrect.

This tool synthesizes data for the VCU Medical Center Main Hospital. If you notice a discrepancy on specific floors (e.g., Level 10 or Level 11)[cite: 1061, 1129], please use the "Feedback" button in TestFlight to take a screenshot of the specific room or zone in question.

3. The app is freezing on the map view.

Because the app renders high-resolution architectural plans, older devices may experience lag. Please ensure you are running the latest version of iOS or Android.
