Privacy Policy

Application: VCU Pharmacy Tool

Last Updated: January 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the VCU Pharmacy Tool ("we," "us," or "the App") collects, uses, and discloses information. This App is intended for use by authorized staff at VCU Medical Center Main Hospital to access facility data and floor plans.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

  • Login Credentials: We collect the username and password you provide strictly to authenticate your access to the application. This data is verified against internal systems and is not stored permanently on the device after the session ends.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: We may collect data about your device model, operating system version (iOS/Android), and screen size to ensure proper rendering of complex floor plans.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous data regarding which floor plans (e.g., Level 10, Level 11) or rooms are accessed most frequently to improve app performance.
  • Crash Logs: During the Beta/TestFlight phase, if the app crashes, we receive technical logs via Apple to help us fix bugs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To grant secure access to restricted facility floor plans and data.
  • To troubleshoot technical issues and resolve application crashes.
  • To improve the user interface and navigation experience for hospital staff.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

  • No Sale of Data: We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.
  • Service Providers: We use Apple TestFlight to distribute beta versions of this app. Apple may collect data regarding your usage and crashes in accordance with their own privacy policy.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. Access to the app is restricted to authorized personnel. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Your Consent

By using the App, you consent to this Privacy Policy. As this is an internal tool, usage is also subject to the internal policies and regulations of VCU Health.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer.
